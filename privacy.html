Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12
At a Glance (TL;DR)
  • Your playlists, stream URLs, server credentials, and watch history never leave your device.
  • We operate zero user tracking databases, host no external user accounts, and sell no personal data.
  • In-app purchases and subscription billing are handled exclusively by Apple Inc.
  • You maintain full control and can delete all local application data directly in Settings.

1. Overview

Your playlists and viewing preferences never leave your device. [BRAND_NAME] is engineered from the ground up on a local-first privacy model. We do not require you to create an account with us to use the player, nor do we operate central backend servers that log your browsing behavior or stream consumption.

This Privacy Policy explains how KTLMA Player (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with your use of the KTLMA Player application across supported Apple platforms.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because KTLMA Player functions as a standalone client playback engine, the following user content and sensitive data are strictly processed and stored locally on your device:

  • Playlist Credentials & URLs: We do not collect, transmit, store, or inspect your M3U playlist URLs, Xtream Codes credentials (username, password, server address), or custom stream addresses.
  • Playback & Viewing History: We do not log what channels you watch, what movies or series you stream, your playback timestamps, or your viewing durations.
  • Personal Identity: We do not collect your name, physical address, email address (unless you voluntarily contact our support team), or phone number.

3. Information Collected

Depending on your device configuration and the optional features you utilize, the following categories of data may be processed:

  • In-App Purchases & Subscriptions: When you purchase an optional Pro subscription, the transaction is processed directly by Apple Inc. through StoreKit. We receive only an anonymous transaction receipt or status token to unlock premium features locally. We never receive or store your credit card or financial account numbers.
  • Advertising Identifiers (If Ads are Enabled): If you use the free, ad-supported tier of the app, Google AdMob and Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework may request permission to use your device Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) to deliver personalized ads. If you deny tracking, only contextual or non-personalized advertisements are displayed.
  • Technical Diagnostics & Crash Data: With your permission via standard Apple diagnostic sharing, anonymous crash reports, system operating version, device model, and playback engine error logs may be collected solely to diagnose bugs and improve app stability.

4. How We Use Information

Any information processed through your use of KTLMA Player is utilized solely for the following limited purposes:

  • To verify and restore your active Pro subscription status across your Apple devices.
  • To maintain, optimize, and improve the performance, stability, and hardware-accelerated playback of the application.
  • To respond promptly to customer support requests and bug reports initiated by you via email.
  • To serve advertisements in compliance with privacy regulations where you have selected the ad-supported tier.

5. Third-Party Services & Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We interact only with vetted platform partners strictly necessary to operate the application:

  • Apple Inc.: Processes in-app purchases, StoreKit transactions, App Store distribution, and optional anonymous crash reporting.
  • RevenueCat: Provides subscription validation infrastructure to verify active Pro entitlements using anonymous App User IDs.
  • Google AdMob: Serves banner, interstitial, and open-app advertisements in the free tier in accordance with Google’s privacy disclosures and your ATT consent settings.

6. Data Retention & Security

All local application data—including saved playlist profiles, parental control PINs, cached channel listings, and favorites—is stored within your device’s secure sandboxed storage. Sensitive server credentials are protected using Apple’s hardware-backed Keychain encryption.

Because we maintain no remote user account database, you can permanently delete all local data, playlists, and settings at any time by going to Settings > Clear All Data, or by uninstalling the app from your device.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and similar privacy laws worldwide, you possess distinct rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access & Portability: All playlist and viewing data is held locally on your device and can be viewed or exported directly through the application interface.
  • Right to Deletion: You can delete all your stored data instantly using in-app settings or by deleting the app.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw ad tracking consent at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > [BRAND_NAME].

If you have questions regarding your privacy rights or wish to exercise them, you may contact us at [SUPPORT_EMAIL].

8. Children’s Privacy

KTLMA Player is not directed to children under the age of thirteen (13), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at [SUPPORT_EMAIL] so that we may take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal requirements. The updated version will be indicated by the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we safeguard your privacy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, comments, or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out to us at Roocothe@proton.me.
