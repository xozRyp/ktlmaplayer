KTLMA Player

KTLMA Player is an independent, high-performance media player engineered to organize, decode, and render user-provided streaming playlists with precision. [BRAND_NAME] does not provide, host, or distribute any media content.

Users connect their own authorized M3U playlists or Xtream Codes servers, enjoying studio-grade hardware decoding, an intelligent electronic program guide, and complete privacy control on Apple platforms.

Core Architecture & Capabilities

Multi-Profile Playlist Management

Switch seamlessly between separate M3U and Xtream accounts with independent channel categorizations and fast cached metadata.

M3U & Xtream Protocol Support

Comprehensive compatibility with HLS (.m3u8), MPEG-TS (.ts), MP4, and MKV streams across live broadcast, movie, and series catalogs.

Favorites & Watch History

Keep your favorite channels, custom channel groups, and recently watched series organized with on-device instant resume bookmarks.

Parental Controls & PIN Security

Protect family viewing with a 4-digit security PIN that locks sensitive categories, adult channels, and configuration settings.

Catch-Up Replay & EPG Grid

Navigate live schedules with an interactive XMLTV program timeline and rewind past broadcasts on provider-supported catch-up channels.

Hardware-Accelerated Engine

Optimized for Apple Silicon with low-latency channel zapping, multi-audio selection, Picture-in-Picture, and variable playback speeds.

Built for Viewers

Local-First Privacy
No external analytics, no user accounts on our servers, and zero telemetry on your streaming habits.
Hardware Acceleration
Direct Apple VideoToolbox GPU decoding for cool thermals and minimal battery drain during extended viewing.
Zero Bundled Content
Pure, neutral playback utility that respects copyright and puts the viewer in complete control of their sources.
