Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add an M3U or Xtream Codes playlist? +
To add your playlist, open [BRAND_NAME] and tap the + icon on the home screen or navigate to Profiles > Add Source. You can choose either M3U Playlist URL or Xtream Codes API. Enter your provider URL, credentials (if using Xtream), and choose an optional profile nickname. The app will parse and organize your live channels, movies, and series locally.
Why is my playlist not loading or showing an error? +
Most playlist loading issues stem from a typo in the URL or temporary server downtime at your provider. Verify that your M3U URL or Xtream server address begins with http:// or https:// and contains no spaces. If your server uses plain HTTP, ensure your connection allows local network media requests. You can also re-add the profile to refresh your cached index.
How can I reduce buffering or stream stutter? +
If streams stutter or buffer frequently:
1. Check your Wi-Fi or cellular bandwidth speed.
2. Open player settings during playback and switch the rendering engine between Hardware Acceleration and Software Compatibility Mode.
3. Verify that your provider stream bandwidth matches your available connection.
Why is my Electronic Program Guide (EPG) missing or out of sync? +
EPG program listings are provided directly by your third-party playlist provider via XMLTV. If program schedules are blank or offset, check your device time zone in iOS Settings, and ensure your provider includes active tvg-id tags for your channel lineup. You can force-refresh the guide in Settings > Refresh TV Guide.
How do I reset my Parental Control PIN? +
If you have forgotten your 4-digit parental control PIN, navigate to Settings > Parental Controls and select Reset Security PIN. If access is locked, you can clear local application security preferences by removing and re-adding your profile in the workspace manager.
Why is Catch-Up replay not working on some channels? +
Catch-Up replay availability depends entirely on whether your playlist provider archives broadcast streams and supports Xtream Catch-Up or archive stream headers (such as timeshift). If a channel lacks catch-up indicators in the program guide, your provider does not support replay on that stream.
How do subscriptions, renewals, and refunds work? +
All subscriptions and in-app purchases are securely processed by Apple Inc. through your Apple ID account:
• Auto-Renewal: Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours prior to the end of the billing period.
• Cancellation: You can cancel your subscription at any time in your iOS Settings (or App Store) > [BRAND_NAME] > Subscriptions.
• Refunds: Refund requests are managed exclusively by Apple Inc. at reportaproblem.apple.com.
• Restore: If you reinstall the app or change devices, tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Pro screen to restore your active access.
What are the device and operating system requirements? +
KTLMA Player requires a recent iOS version on iPhone and iPod touch, iPadOS on iPad, or macOS on Apple Silicon Macs. A persistent internet connection is required to fetch and stream your playlists.
